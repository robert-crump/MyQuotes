2026-10-15 — Issue #52: Hashtag suggestions need strong reasons

The rules come from a /grill-me session (see the issue). `HashtagSuggester` no longer uses log-lift. A word is a reason for a tag when ≥ 4 quotes contain it and have the tag (`MIN_WORD_WITH_TAG`), and that is ≥ 50% of the quotes containing it (`MIN_SHARE_PERCENT`). The author is a reason when ≥ 2 of their quotes have the tag (`MIN_AUTHOR_WITH_TAG`) at ≥ 50%; this replaces the 3-quote/60% exception. A tag on ≥ 5 quotes is suggested on a single reason. The top 3 are ranked by each tag's strongest reason (share, then count, compared in integers; `STRONGEST_FIRST` for lists), then by tag name. `explain` and `profile` use the same `isReason`. `profile` lists every reason word and author, with no top-5 cap, and `supportingTokens`, `PROFILE_*`, `MIN_SUPPORTING_TOKENS` and `AUTHOR_MIN_*` are gone. `HashtagSuggestionText`: counts as "(6/9 quotes)" on the first item of a list and "(4/7)" after it. `INTRO` is the dialog's new top text, and the "(6 of 8)" footnote is removed. `suggestions(profile)` builds the sheet's "Not suggested yet" text, or its Words paragraph ("No word is strong enough yet (needs 4 quotes and at least half).") and an optional Author paragraph. `HashtagSheet` fills one `sheet_suggestions` container. The status view and the per-word lines are gone. CONTEXT.md updated. HashtagSuggesterTest (22) and HashtagSuggestionTextTest (5) were rewritten for thresholds, ranking, ordering and texts, and all unit tests pass.

2026-09-30 — Issue #50: Hashtag suggestions on Add/Edit, learned from the collection

New pure `HashtagSuggester`: `fit(quotes)` and `suggest(text, author, source, existingTags)`. Tokens are the quote text lowercased and split on non-letters, with words under 3 letters and built-in English/German stopwords dropped, cut to a 6-letter stem, plus one `author:<name>` token (the source is not used). Each quote counts a token once. A tag's score is the sum of log-lift P(w|t)/P(w) with add-one smoothing over the supporting tokens (tokens seen with the tag that have positive lift). A token never seen with the tag doesn't count, because smoothing alone would give every unseen word positive lift. Constants: `MIN_QUOTES_PER_TAG` 5, `MIN_SUPPORTING_TOKENS` 2, the author exception (≥ 3 quotes, `AUTHOR_MIN_PERCENT` 60, compared in integers), `MAX_SUGGESTIONS` 3, ties broken by name, existing tags excluded ignoring case, and `supportingTokens` explains a suggestion. The issue's example `liebe`/`lieben` → `liebe` doesn't hold with the 6-letter stem it asks for (`lieben` has 6 letters). I kept 6 (so `friendship`/`friendships` → `friend` works), and the German test uses `Freundschaft`/`Freunde` → `freund`. UI: the `hashtag_suggestions` row under the chip field ("Suggested:" plus outlined `Chip.Assist` chips with a + from `chip_hashtag_suggestion`), hidden when empty. `HashtagSuggestionRow` refits on a single background thread whenever the Quote Collection emits, excluding the edited quote's id. It refreshes 300 ms after the author/quote text last changed and at once on chip changes (the new `HashtagChipField.setOnTagsChangedListener`), and a tap goes through `HashtagChipField.addTag`. It is released in `AddEditActivity.onDestroy`. CONTEXT.md has a "Hashtag suggestions" entry. `HashtagSuggesterTest` has 12 tests for everything the issue lists, and all unit tests pass.

Checked on the Pixel_10_Pro emulator (API 37) with the 32-quote demo collection. Typing "Truth is simple and pure" showed "Suggested: #Truth #Humor". Tapping #Truth made it a chip and left #Humor in the row. Timing on the desktop JVM with 5,000 synthetic quotes and 40 tags: fit took 45–110 ms (background thread) and one suggest about 0.2 ms (main thread). Not checked by hand: Edit mode on the device (the exclusion is covered by a unit test), the author exception in the UI, dark mode, and a real collection of thousands of quotes on a phone.
